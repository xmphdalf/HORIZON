# About the science

Horizon lets you adjust screen brightness and tint for comfort. Its controls target the screen. Room lighting, the timing of exposure, and the time spent using a device also matter when interpreting research on light and sleep.

Brown et al. give recommendations for daytime, evening, and nighttime light exposure in healthy adults. Duraccio et al. studied a phone’s Night Shift setting in young adults and found no significant difference in sleep outcomes across the three groups overall. Neither study tests Horizon or establishes a therapeutic setting for its presets.

- [Brown et al., PLOS Biology (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3001571)
- [Duraccio et al., Sleep Health (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sleh.2021.03.005)
