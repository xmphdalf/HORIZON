# Changelog

## 1.0.0-rc1 · Android test build

- Added manual warmth and brightness controls, RGB tints, and editable presets.
- Added fixed-time and solar schedules, gradual transitions, and per-app exclusions.
- Added notification controls, two widget sizes, a Quick Settings tile, and a launcher shortcut to turn the filter off.
- Rebalanced the factory presets and adjusted how the overlay and system brightness share the dimming.
- Added a permanent **Why Horizon is free** link on Home with optional one-time Google Play tips.
